This file lists a bunch of useful SDL functions.

SDL_CreateWindow();

SDL_GetWindowSurface();
returns a pointer to SDL_Surface the window's surface

SDL_LoadBMP();

SDL_BlitSurface();
copies the surface from source to the destination

SDL_FillRect();
fills the surface rectangle with a specific color

SDL_ConvertSurface();
converts a surface's format to the source's format

SDL_BlitScaled();
blits a surface scaled to the specified SDL_Rect<x,y,w,h>

SDL_FreeSurface();

SDL_DestroyWindow();

SDL_Quit();
